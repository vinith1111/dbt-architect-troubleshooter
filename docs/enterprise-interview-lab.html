Case study: Mainframe to Snowflake

A fixed-length mainframe feed delivers around 500 million records and 1–2 TB daily. Three years of history must be loaded, followed by daily incremental processing. Bronze → Silver → Gold is required, and an existing Power BI/Tableau presentation model must remain compatible.

Clarify first: Is 1–2 TB compressed or uncompressed? Are the historical files available? What is the SLA? What are the record layout, encoding, business keys, update/delete semantics and source control totals?

Scale check: 500 million × 365 × 3 ≈ 547.5 billion records. At 1–2 TB/day, raw input is roughly 1.1–2.2 PB across three years, before compression or retention effects.

15–25 minExplain backfill, parallel daily capture and cutover.
25–35 minHandle failures, reconciliation, performance and cost.
35–42 minDiscuss BI compatibility, monitoring and operational ownership.
42–45 minSummarize trade-offs, risks and next validation steps.

Strong candidates call out assumptions, distinguish facts from estimates, and propose a benchmark rather than promising an unmeasured load time.
